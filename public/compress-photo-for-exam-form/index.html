Every exam publishes its own photo rules: size in pixels or centimetres, a KB range and a file type. Read your notification first, then set the same values in FormReady. Every value can be edited.

How it works

  1. Find the photo size and KB limit in your exam notification.
  2. Tap the button and enter those values with "Edit size".
  3. Choose your photo and centre your face.
  4. Download and upload to the exam portal.
