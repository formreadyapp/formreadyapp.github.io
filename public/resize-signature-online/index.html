Resize my signature

Forms usually ask for a small, wide signature image with a strict KB limit. Sign on plain white paper with a dark pen, take a clear photo, and FormReady will crop and compress it.

How it works

  1. Sign on white paper with a black or dark blue pen.
  2. Take a straight photo of the signature in good light.
  3. Tap the button, choose the photo, and drag to centre the signature.
  4. Check the KB limit in your form and download.

Good to know

  • Your files stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Your first download in each tool is free, then ₹10 per download.
  • Rules differ between forms, so always check your official notification.
